PROMPT · SCENE 4  —  Claude Code
================================

Build Scene 4, the fall. Assets: kodak-plant-bg.png (a shuttered Kodak film plant — boarded windows, a faded yellow-and-red sign, chain-link) and grim-exec.png (a cut-out of a defeated exec in a suit, box of desk things under one arm). Both are flat plates — sell depth by animating them, not by adding layers. Anchor everything to one ground point at the man's feet (about x560 / y1690). Timings (30fps):

- Grounded parallax punch: push the plant from scale 1.0 to 1.12 over frames 8–80, easing out. Over frames 0–58 punch the man in harder — up to about 1.7× and drifting toward camera — scaling him around his feet, not the frame centre. The man growing faster than the wall is the whole depth illusion.

- The shadow is the man: duplicate the grim-exec cut-out, paint it pure black, flip it straight down from his feet and skew it about −53° so it lies along the ground; blur it 7px and drop it to 55% opacity. No separate shadow asset.

- Left line: stack "filed for / chapter / eleven" in serif italic on the left from frame 24, one line every ~6 frames, then fade it back to about 30% as the man moves.

- The shift: around frame 60 slide the man ~235px to the left with a motion-blur streak that peaks mid-move, clearing the right side.

- Right line: reveal "the film plants went dark" on the right over frames 70–78, draw a hand-drawn yellow underline on beneath it, and tighten a focus vignette around it so the eye lands there.

- The last light: on frame 96 kill a bank of windows on the plant — a soft warm rectangle of light behind the glass that snaps to black on a hold keyframe, no fade. One frame, and the building is dead.

- Grade: desaturate this one further than the others — saturate 0.6, contrast 1.12, sepia 0.1, brightness 0.92 — for the sombre beat. Then the shared film treatment and the 12fps posterize step.
